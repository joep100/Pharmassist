How a delivery runs

Three steps, and two of them are ours

Nothing about your dispensing process changes. The only new thing is a bag going into a different pair of hands.

01

You dispense and seal

Exactly as you do today. Your pharmacist checks the bag, seals it and labels it with the patient's name and Eircode. That's your part finished.

02

We collect

Book the job and a courier calls to your counter. Bags are scanned out of your shop and into our care, so there's a record of the handover from the start.

03

The patient has it today

Delivered to the door, with the recipient confirmed and a timestamped proof of delivery back to you. Nobody waits until tomorrow.

Where the line sits

Three things we don't do

Most delivery services want to become the pharmacy. We're the opposite, and we'd rather be blunt about it up front.

We don't dispense

You dispense, you check, you seal. We carry a closed bag and we don't open it. Professional responsibility for what's inside stays with your pharmacist, where it belongs.

We don't prescribe or advise

No clinical questions, no substitutions, no counselling at the door. If a patient asks a driver something clinical, the answer is your phone number.

We don't take your patient

No marketplace, no competing pharmacy, no app that quietly moves them somewhere cheaper. They arrived as your patient and they stay your patient.

Why pharmacies use us

Same day is the one thing the post can't do

Answer the online-only players

Mail-order pharmacies compete on convenience and win on it. They cannot get a bag across Dublin this afternoon. You can, and you're already round the corner.

Clear the collection shelf

Bags that sit waiting are money tied up and stock you can't shift. Offering delivery turns a shelf of uncollected prescriptions into completed sales.

Serve the patients who can't come in

Housebound patients, parents with a sick child, anyone mid-shift. Today that's a staff member in their own car for an hour. It doesn't need to be.

Independently certified

Audited, not just promised

Handing patient medicines to somebody else is a real decision, and it shouldn't have to rest on our word. Our operation is certified by the NSAI to ISO 9001 for quality management and ISO 14001 for environmental management.

In practice that means the way we handle, track and hand over your bags is written down, followed, and checked by an auditor from outside the company. It's also the paperwork your own procedures will want to see.
